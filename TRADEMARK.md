# The Bookplate name and logo

The Bookplate **code** is open source under the Apache License 2.0: you may
use, change, redistribute and host it, commercially or not.

The **name "Bookplate" and the Bookplate logo** are not part of that license
(Apache 2.0, section 6). That means:

- ✅ You can say your service or fork is "based on Bookplate" or "powered by
  Bookplate".
- ✅ You can use the name to refer to this project, write about it, or
  package it unmodified (e.g. in a NAS app store).
- ❌ Don't offer a modified version, or a hosted service, *as* "Bookplate",
  or use the logo in a way that suggests it's the official project.

If you're unsure, open an issue and ask — the answer will usually be yes.
